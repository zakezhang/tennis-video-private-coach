How it works工作原理

From phone footage to a coaching review

从手机视频到教练式复盘

Claude runs the analysis in Python with an open pose model, then checks the key frames by eye before anything goes into the report.

Claude 用 Python 和开源姿态模型做分析，进入报告的关键帧都会再逐帧人工确认。

STEP 1Pose姿态识别

YOLO pose on every few frames; you are tracked by your clothing if others are in shot.

YOLO 姿态模型；画面里有别人时按衣服颜色跟踪你。

STEP 2Swings找挥拍

Peaks in wrist speed, at least 1.6 s apart; hand position before the swing tells forehand from backhand.

手腕速度峰值，间隔至少 1.6 秒；按挥拍前手在身体哪侧分正反手。

STEP 3Contact击球时刻

The sound of the strings, filtered to skip the bounce off the wall.

用击球声音定位，避开球撞墙的声音。

STEP 4Metrics测指标

Take-back height, knee bend, stance width, contact height, one-leg landings.

引拍高度、屈膝、站距、击球高度、单脚落地。

STEP 5Clips做视频

Best and worst shots picked, checked frame by frame, and annotated.

挑出最好和最差的拍子，逐帧确认后加标注。

STEP 6Plan练习计划

Faults mapped to drills for your setting, with targets for next time.

把问题对应到适合你场景的练习，并定下次的目标。

Filming拍摄

Better footage, sharper review

拍得越清楚，复盘越准

  • Side-on is the most useful angle: outside the net on your hitting side, 3–4 m away, at waist height.
  • 侧面机位最有用：持拍手一侧网外 3–4 米、腰高。
  • Behind you, 1.2–1.5 m high and centered, is best for counting net and out.
  • 身后机位 1.2–1.5 米高、正对中线，最适合统计过网/下网。
  • 4K 60 fps or 1080p 120 fps, focus and exposure locked, a few continuous minutes rather than many short clips.
  • 4K 60 帧或 1080p 120 帧，锁定对焦和曝光，连续拍几分钟，不要拍很多短片段。
  • Outdoors, frame more of the far court and less sky and trees.
  • 室外少拍天空和树，多拍对面场地。

Limits. Pose comes from one phone camera, so angles are estimates. Compare with your own past sessions, not with other players. This is not medical advice; if something keeps hurting, see a sports-medicine doctor.

局限。姿态来自单个手机机位，角度只是估计，适合和自己的历次数据比，不适合和别人比。这不是医疗建议，持续疼痛请去看运动医学科。
